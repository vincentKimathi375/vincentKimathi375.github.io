WEB DEVELOPMENT & E-COMMERCE

Flopaty Perfumes – Business Website

Designed and developed a responsive e-commerce website for Flopaty Perfumes, a perfume and fashion accessories business based in Mpeketoni, Lamu County, Kenya.

The website allows customers to create accounts, explore products, browse categories, add items to their shopping cart and place orders directly through WhatsApp.

Technologies: HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and MySQL.
